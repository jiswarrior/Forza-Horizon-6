Legend Island — How to Unlock & What's Inside

Legend Island is the endgame area of Forza Horizon 6 — a separate island in the southeast corner of the Japan map that stays locked until you earn the Gold Wristband (Horizon Legend status). It's the only part of the FH6 map that requires progression to access.

How to unlock Legend Island
Earn the Gold Wristband by accumulating 32,500 total Festival Points and completing the Gold Wristband event. This is the highest tier of the 7-Wristband progression system.

Fastest way to reach the Gold Wristband

1
Prioritise Story chapters (yellow badge icons)
These give the most Festival Points per minute of any activity. Complete every yellow badge event on the map before grinding races.
2
Complete Wristband Events immediately when they unlock
Each Wristband Event gives a large Festival Points chunk. Complete them as soon as they appear — don't delay.
3
Diversify your race types
Your 50th Road Race gives far fewer points than your 1st Dirt Race. Switch disciplines regularly — Cross Country, Drag, Time Attack, Street Races all give disproportionate points when new.
4
Play Horizon Play online (levels 1–25 give Festival Points)
Every Horizon Play online match earns XP. The first 25 level-ups each grant Festival Points directly into your Wristband progress.
5
Smash XP Boards as you drive between events
Each board gives 10–50 Wristband points. Not a huge source individually but adds up passively.

What's on Legend Island

⚑ The Colossus
The longest race in Forza Horizon history — loops the entire Japanese freeway system in a single epic run. Each lap takes 10+ minutes. An AFK farming method using AutoDrive on the Colossus is the best passive Credits and XP farm in the late game.
🏎 Legend Island Circuit
An exclusive circuit race course not available anywhere else on the main Japan map. Hosts exclusive R-Class events and leaderboard challenges.
📋 New XP Boards
A full fresh set of XP Boards exclusive to Legend Island — untouched and worth collecting for their Wristband point bonuses and XP.
🏆 Exclusive Rewards
Unique car rewards and achievements tied to Legend Island content. Completing the Colossus race and Legend Island Circuit each unlock separate achievements worth 20G each.
🎪 Legend Island Festival Outpost
A secondary festival hub exclusive to Horizon Legends. Acts as a fast travel point and hosts exclusive seasonal events and meet-ups.
💰 Best AFK Farm in the game
Colossus + AutoDrive + Unbeatable difficulty (no opponents) = +65% Credits bonus per run. Best overnight Credits and XP farm in FH6. See the Credits farming guide for setup.
Manage your expectations
Legend Island is rewarding for completionists and end-game content hunters, but it isn't an overwhelming new area. It's a supplementary festival outpost — the real reward is the Gold Wristband achievement (50G) and the AFK farming opportunity. Don't neglect the main Japan map content in a rush to get here.
