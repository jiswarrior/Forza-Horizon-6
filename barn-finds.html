Barn Finds are back in Forza Horizon 6 — 15 hidden classic and racing cars scattered across Japan, from mountain forests to coastal roads. Unlike previous Forza Horizon games, Barn Finds are not tied to Wristband progression. They use an entirely separate system: the Discover Japan Stamps in your Collection Journal.

Critical — visit order matters
If you drive to a barn's location before earning the required Stamp level, nothing will happen — the barn door will not open and the car will not appear. You must unlock the Stamp rumor first, then go to the search zone.

How the Discover Japan Stamp system works

There are 7 Stamp levels, each unlocking a new batch of Barn Find rumors on your map. Stamps are earned through exploration activities — not racing. Here is the progression path:

1
Earn Discover Japan points
Complete Story chapters (yellow badge icons — highest yield per minute), Street Races, Touge Battles, food delivery jobs, and smash Regional Mascots. All exploration activities count.
2
Reach a new Stamp level
Check progress anytime: Campaign Menu → Collection Journal → Discover Japan. Seven levels total — Visitor (1,250 pts) through Master Explorer (20,000 pts).
3
Barn Find rumors appear on your map
Each new Stamp unlocks 1–4 Barn Find search zones, shown as large purple circles. 2–4 Barn Finds unlock per level, depending on the tier.
4
Search the purple zone for the barn
Look for dirt tracks branching off roads inside the zone. Use ANNA Drone Mode to fly around and spot the barn roof faster. Most barns are slightly off the main road.
5
Wait for restoration — or pay Credits
After the discovery cutscene, the car is taken to a mechanic. Restoration takes several hours of play time. Pay Credits to speed it up. Mei will notify you when it's ready.
Fastest stamp farming method
Story chapters (yellow badge activities in the Collection Journal) give the most Discover Japan points per minute. Prioritise these. Street Races and Touge Battles give good points too and also progress the Horizon Play multiplayer system simultaneously.

Detailed locations

01
2005 Honda NSX-R GT
Yellow — VisitorOhtani
Head to the river running south of the Ohtani region. Find the curvy road with two hairpin turns, then follow the dirt path up the hill. The barn is tucked near the hilltop — look for the Kinkaku-ji Temple point of interest to the northwest of the zone as a reference landmark.
★ Your first Barn Find. An iconic Japanese sports car — fitting opener for a game set in Japan.
02
1969 Toyota 2000GT
Green — SightseerIto
Southern Ito, near the seawall. Head past the Minka House toward the beach in northern Ito, then follow the edge of the search zone south toward the dock. Go past the Satta Sprint race area and down the access road to find the barn tucked between trees near the coastal edge.
One of the most historically significant Japanese cars ever made. A cornerstone collector's piece.
03
1992 Ford Sierra Cosworth RS500
Green — SightseerIto
Also in the Ito region. Look near the northern edge of the search zone, off a dirt track branching from the main coastal road heading inland. The barn sits in a small clearing in the forest.
04
1971 Nissan Skyline 2000GT-R
Blue — TravellerNangan
South of the Nangan search zone on the main road. Drive past the houses and just before the fork in the road, a dirt path branches off to the right — follow it directly to the barn.
The great-grandfather of all GT-Rs. A must-have for any Skyline fan.
05
1987 Nissan Pao
Blue — TravellerMinamino
Minamino region, north-eastern edge of the search zone. Follow a dirt track from the main road — the barn is at the end of the path in the forest.
The most unusual find on the list. The Pao is a retro city car — no racing pedigree, but a genuine collector's gem.
06
1982 Porsche 911 Turbo 3.3
Pink — PathfinderOhtani
Ohtani region, in a bamboo forest next to a dirt road between the two main roads near Mei's house. Head southwest from Mei's house and find the dirt path leading south just before the intersection. The barn is visible from the path.
The classic narrow-body Porsche — rear-engined, treacherous in bends, legendary on straights.
07
1984 Peugeot 205 Turbo 16
Pink — PathfinderShimanoyama
Shimanoyama region, northwestern part of the map. The dirt path appears just before the Narai-Juku main road and leads up a short hill to the barn in the woods to the north.
The legendary WRC homologation special. One of the fastest rally cars ever built.
08
1962 Lincoln Continental
Pink — PathfinderHokubu
Hokubu region, center of the search zone. Look southwest of the Shikisai-No-Aka flower fields. Follow the orange road cutting through the fields heading southwest, take the next left onto a dirt track — the barn is tucked into a patch of trees just south of the field boundary.
An American icon with iconic suicide doors. The most stylish car on the entire Barn Find list.
09
1998 Nissan #23 Pennzoil NISMO Skyline GT-R
Pink — PathfinderTakashiro
Takashiro region, eastern edge of the search zone. Drive north along the white mountain road directly east of the waterfall and shrine (near the Waterfall Trail dirt race). Look for a gravel road and car park on the right with a building at the end — the barn is behind it.
★ The iconic Pennzoil JGTC racer. One of the most visually striking cars in the game. The 1995 Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution III GSR Treasure Car is also near the waterfall — grab both in one trip.
10
1995 Lamborghini Diablo SV
Orange — NavigatorOhtani
Ohtani region, border area near Shimanoyama. Follow a dirt track on the north-eastern edge of the search zone. Also accessible via the main road north-east, near the Bridge Underpass Trailblazer start gate.
11
1997 Mitsubishi Montero Evolution
Orange — NavigatorShimanoyama
Shimanoyama region. From the Narai-Juku Circuit Race event, drive up the dirt road then turn right onto a path. The barn is visible over the hill. Note: it is a direct neighbour to the Peugeot 205 Turbo 16 Barn Find location.
12
1997 Nissan R390 GT1
Purple — AdventurerIto — north
Northernmost Barn Find in the Ito area, near the coastline. Follow the dirt track north of the Matsumi Great Bridge — the barn is alongside the coastal road near two offroad tracks that split from the main route.
★ Nissan's only serious attempt at Le Mans. An extremely rare racing machine.
13
Mitsubishi #1 Lancer Evolution Time Attack
Purple — AdventurerShimanoyama
Shimanoyama region, almost directly in the center of the search zone overlooking Narai-Juku town to the south. Take the high dirt road from the Narai-Juku Circuit road race event, then go down the path on the right where the barn sits over the hill. Close to both the Peugeot 205 T16 location and the Porsche 959 Treasure Car.
14
Nissan #11 Tomica Skyline Turbo Super Silhouette
Gold — Master ExplorerIto
Ito region, southwestern edge of the search area. Near where the main road enters the zone from the west, there is a wide dirt path leading south between some distinctively thin trees — the barn is visible from this path and is one of the easier Gold-tier finds to spot on the map.
A legendary Japanese touring car racing silhouette — one of the most dramatic-looking cars in the game.
15
Mazda #55 Mazda 787B
Gold — Master ExplorerTakashiro
Takashiro region — the most difficult Barn Find in the game despite the open environment. The barn is in the woods directly north of the search zone, hidden behind buildings, hiking trails, and farmland. Take the dirt track on the north-western edge of the zone from the main road. Also reachable from the main road north-east of the area, near the Bridge Underpass Trailblazer start gate.
★★ The crown jewel of FH6 Barn Finds. The 787B won the 1991 24 Hours of Le Mans — the only Japanese car ever to do so. Its rotary Wankel engine sounds unlike anything else in the game. Note: instant restoration costs seven figures in Credits — plan ahead or let the timer run.

After you find a barn

Once a car is collected, the barn pin on your map changes and that location converts into a Gift Drop spot — you can use it to share cars with other players online. The restored car is added permanently to your garage. All 15 Barn Find cars are separate from the 9 Treasure Cars also hidden across Japan.

Treasure Map DLC
The paid Treasure Map DLC marks all Barn Find and Treasure Car locations on your map automatically. However, you still need the required Stamp level to trigger the find — the DLC only reveals where the barn is, not bypasses the unlock requirement.
