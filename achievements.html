Missable achievements

Forza Horizon 6 launches with 57 achievements worth a total of 1,000 Gamerscore on Xbox and PC (Steam). The PlayStation 5 version launches later in 2026 and will include an equivalent trophy list plus a Platinum trophy. No achievements appear to be missable — every unlock condition can be completed at any point during your playthrough.

Key insight for efficient completion
Wristbands and Stamps account for roughly 300G combined. Running both tracks in parallel is dramatically more efficient than finishing one before starting the other — every Street Race you do counts toward both your Wristband Festival Points and your Discover Japan Stamp progress simultaneously.

1000G roadmap — four phases

1
Early game — natural unlocks (~200G)
Just play normally through the Prologue and first Wristbands
  • Complete the Opening Act (Prologue drive through Tokyo) — 10G
  • Earn Yellow Wristband (complete Horizon Qualifiers + Invitational) — 10G
  • Win your first Horizon Festival race event — 10G
  • Earn Green and Blue Wristbands naturally through racing — 10G each
  • Claim your first Barn Find — 10G
  • Find your first Treasure Car — 10G
  • Complete your first Touge Battle — 10G
  • Earn your first Discover Japan Stamp (Visitor) — 10G
  • Own 25 cars in your garage — 10G
  • Complete first Food Delivery job — 10G
2
Mid game — progression milestones (~350G)
Wristbands 4–6, Stamps 3–5, collection goals
  • Earn Pink, Orange, Purple Wristbands — 10–20G each
  • Earn Stamps through Pathfinder, Navigator, Adventurer — 10G each
  • Win 57 Horizon Festival race events — 30G (grind milestone)
  • Own 100 cars — 30G (major grind — use Wheelspins and Auction House)
  • Smash 100 XP Boards — 10G
  • Smash 100 Regional Mascots — 10G
  • Complete all 15 Barn Finds — 30G
  • Complete all 9 Treasure Cars — 20G (Treasure Hunter)
  • Reveal 50% of the Japan map — 20G
  • Play any Horizon Play online mode — 10G
  • Win a Spec Racing event — 10G
  • Win a Touge Showdown match — 10G
3
Late game — endgame unlocks (~300G)
Gold Wristband, Master Explorer, map completion
  • Horizon Legend — earn the Gold Wristband — 50G (biggest single reward)
  • Master Explorer — earn the Gold Stamp — 50G (tied with Horizon Legend)
  • Reveal the full Japan map (Horizon Cartographer) — 30G
  • Complete Legend Island's Colossus race — 20G
  • Smash all 200 XP Boards — 30G (from 100 milestone)
  • Smash all 200 Regional Mascots — 30G (from 100 milestone)
  • Earn all Festival Playlist points for a season — 30G (requires full Series completion)
4
Cleanup — miscellaneous & online (~150G)
Mop up remaining activities and online requirements
  • Win The Eliminator — 15G (luck + skill required)
  • Find a player using Hide & Seek — 10G
  • Complete a LINK Skill chain with another player — 10G
  • Visit another player's Estate — 10G
  • Complete a Spec Racing championship — 20G
  • Complete all Discover Japan Story chapters — 20G
  • Set a PR Stunt record in each category — 20G

Hardest achievements to watch for
Playlist Complete (30G) requires finishing all Festival Playlist points for an entire Series — that's 4 weeks minimum. Start immediately. Board Destroyer (30G) and Mascot Master (30G) are the longest grinds — 200 each. Eliminator (15G) requires winning the battle royale mode which is heavily luck-dependent.

PS5 trophy note

The PlayStation 5 version of FH6 arrives later in 2026. It will include an equivalent list of trophies plus a Platinum trophy for collecting all others, bringing the total to 58 trophies on PS5. Cross-save between Xbox, PC, and PS5 is supported — the first time in the series. Progress carries over between platforms.
