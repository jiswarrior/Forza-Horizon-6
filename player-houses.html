FH6 Player Houses — All 8 Locations, Prices & Perks

Updated May 22, 2026~8 min readAll 8 confirmed

1

10K CR
Cheapest purchase (Yashiki)

Player Houses in FH6 are more than cosmetic garages. Each house gives a passive perk that activates permanently once you buy it — extra Credits, daily Wheelspins, Autoshow discounts, Estate building access, and more. There are 8 houses across Japan, all tied to Discover Japan Stamp progression except the first two.

Unlocking ≠ owning
Reaching a new Stamp level unlocks the ability to buy a house — it doesn't give it to you. You still need to spend Credits. Check your Collection Journal → Discover Japan to see which houses are available to purchase.

Best purchase order

You don't need to buy all 8 — focus on perks that match your playstyle. Here's the priority order most players recommend:

  1. Mei's House — Free, claim immediately for Wheeler Dealer perk
  2. Yashiki House — Only 10,000 CR, unlocks Estate building for skill farming
  3. Tokyo House — Daily Wheelspin is the best passive income perk in the game (free with VIP)
  4. Minka House — Extra Credits from Stunt events if you do Speed Zones/Danger Signs
  5. Vision House — Two perks, but requires Gold Stamp and 1.5M CR

All 8 player houses

01
Mei's House
FREE No stamp required
Wheeler Dealer
Lets you trade cars at the Autoshow — buy, sell and swap vehicles with other players
Located on the main road north of Tokyo City in the Ohtani region. Unlocks automatically through early campaign progression — no purchase needed.
02
Yashiki House (The Estate)
10,000 CR Yellow stamp — Visitor
Estate Builder
Unlocks the Estate building system — create custom race tracks, drift zones, stunt parks, and car meet spaces using EventLab tools. Also the base for the best Skill Point / XP farming method in the game.
Located in the Hokubu region, west of the flower fields. At 10,000 CR it's the cheapest purchasable house and almost always worth buying immediately after your first Stamp.
03
Tokyo House
3,000,000 CR FREE with VIP Pass No stamp needed
Daily Wheelspin
Get one free Wheelspin every day you play. The best passive income perk in the game — a daily Wheelspin adds up to hundreds of thousands of Credits and free cars over a season.
Located in Tokyo City. Expensive at 3M CR, but free for Premium Edition / VIP Pass owners. If you're on Game Pass Ultimate, you likely already have VIP and can claim this for free immediately.
04
Minka House
~200,000 CR Blue stamp — Traveller
Credit Stunts
Earn extra Credits from Horizon Stunt Party events — Speed Traps, Speed Zones, Drift Zones, and Danger Signs. Ideal if you regularly hunt PR Stunts.
Located in the Ito region. Available after Blue stamp (Traveller). Worth buying if you actively hunt PR Stunts for Credits and Discover Japan points simultaneously.
05
Hakusan Mountain Lodge
~500,000 CR Pink stamp — Pathfinder
Touge Specialist
Earn extra Credits and XP from Street Races and Touge Battle events. Best perk for players who enjoy the mountain road racing side of the game.
Located in the Shimanoyama mountain area, near the Narai-Juku heritage site. Available after Pink stamp (Pathfinder).
06
Fuji Unkai House
~750,000 CR Orange stamp — Navigator
Career Driver
Earn an extra 10% Credits when completing Horizon Festival jobs and championships. Stacks with other credit bonuses including difficulty multipliers.
Located near Mt. Fuji / Sotoyama region, with a view above the cloud line. Available after Orange stamp (Navigator).
07
Soko 78
980,000 CR Purple stamp — Adventurer
Import / Export
Reduces Autoshow car prices by 5%. Sounds small but adds up — a 5M CR hypercar costs 250,000 CR less. Best perk for collectors who regularly buy cars from the Autoshow.
Located in Tokyo City. A converted warehouse / urban property available after Purple stamp (Adventurer). Second most expensive standard house.
08
Vision House
1,500,000 CR Gold stamp — Master Explorer
Driving With Friends + Additional Garage Slot
Two perks: earn extra 10% skill score from LINK Skills when in a convoy, plus one additional car display slot in your garage. The only house with two perks.
Located in the Ohtani region. The final and most beautiful house, requiring both Gold stamp and 1.5M CR. Two perks make it worth the investment for social players and completionists.
