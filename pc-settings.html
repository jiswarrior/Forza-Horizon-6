FH6 Best PC Settings — Graphics & Performance Guide

Updated May 22, 2026ForzaTech engineDLSS 4.5 · FSR 3.1 · XeSS 2.0

FH6 uses an updated ForzaTech engine with a proven track record on mid- and low-tier hardware. The game is well optimised at launch — the minimum spec card is an Nvidia GTX 1650 (three GPU generations old), meaning most PCs can run it. This guide covers the settings that matter most for performance, what to lower first, and how to set up upscaling correctly.

Use the built-in benchmark first
FH6 includes a real-time VRAM and RAM usage counter, plus a built-in benchmark tool in graphics settings. Run it before manually tweaking anything — the game will suggest a preset based on your hardware. Use that as your starting point.

Upscaling — most important setting

Choosing the right upscaling technology is the single most impactful settings decision. FH6 supports DLSS 4.5 (Nvidia), FSR 3.1 (all GPUs, upgradeable to FSR 4.1 via AMD driver override), and XeSS 2.0 (Intel).

  • Nvidia GPU → DLSS Quality (or DLAA at native for best image quality). DLSS Quality gives ~37% FPS boost over TAA while remaining near-identical visually. Avoid Frame Generation unless your base FPS stays above 60.
  • AMD GPU → FSR Quality. The best general option for non-Nvidia cards. FSR 4.1 via driver override improves image quality further if you have a recent AMD card.
  • Intel GPU → XeSS 2.0. Good performance on Arc cards with minimal quality loss.
  • Upscaling mode by resolution: Quality for 1080p, Balanced for 1440p, Performance for 4K.

VRAM target
FH6 uses nearly 8 GB of VRAM at 1440p even at the lowest texture settings. Keep your GPU VRAM usage below 80% for stable performance. If you have 8 GB VRAM, set Environment Texture to High (not Ultra) to avoid the memory leak issue that causes stuttering over long sessions.

Recommended settings by hardware tier

  • High-end (RTX 4080+, RX 7900 XT+) — Extreme preset, DLAA or DLSS Quality, RT GI Medium, RT Reflections Low. Target: 4K/60 or 1440p/120+
  • Mid-range (RTX 3070/4060, RX 6700 XT) — High preset, DLSS/FSR Quality, Shadow High, Volumetric Fog Medium, RT off. Target: 1440p/60+
  • Budget (GTX 1650, RX 5500 XT) — Medium preset, FSR Balanced, Shadow Medium, RT fully off, Environment Texture Medium. Target: 1080p/30–60
